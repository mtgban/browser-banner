# BANner for Star City Games

**SCG BANner** is BANner's panel on Star City Games.

- **On your orders** (`starcitygames.com`, *My Account*, *Orders*) you pick a
  year, every year, or the last 30, 90 or 120 days, and download a CSV of
  those orders, one line each: order number, date, status, items, refunded,
  subtotal, discount, shipping, tax and total. Star City Games lists an
  order at what was left to pay after store credit, so each order is opened
  for what it really came to. Refunded cards are not counted, and an order
  refunded whole is all zeros. Opening takes a little over a second an
  order; keep the page open until it is done.
- **On an order** it sends the order's cards to BAN, at what each was paid,
  or saves them as CSV. Refunded copies, other games and sealed products are
  left out, and the panel says how many.

It reads the pages with the session your browser already has, so it needs no
permission.
